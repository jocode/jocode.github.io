Privacy Policy — modoinge-metrics

Effective date: October 18, 2026

modoinge-metrics is a private tool operated by Johan Mosquera for his own content channel, Modo Ingeniero (@modoinge). It has a single user: the channel owner. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

Only data from the owner's own accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok, after the owner authorizes it: basic profile information (account ID, username), the list of the owner's own posts, and their statistics (for example views, likes, comments, shares, saves and watch time). It may also read comments on the owner's own posts so the owner can review and answer them.

How the data is used

To measure the performance of the channel's videos and decide what to improve. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling of other people or any other purpose.

Where it is stored

Statistics are stored as files on the owner's own computer. Access tokens are stored in the computer's operating-system keychain. No data is sent to any server operated by the tool.

Sharing

Data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties. The only external services contacted are the official APIs of the platforms themselves (Google/YouTube, Meta/Instagram, TikTok).

Retention and deletion

The owner can delete the stored files at any time and revoke access from each platform's settings (Google Account → Security → Third-party access; Instagram → Settings → Apps and websites; TikTok → Settings → Security → Manage app permissions).

Política de privacidad — modoinge-metrics
